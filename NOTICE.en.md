# Data provenance · 数据与素材的出处

This file is a supplementary explanation to [LICENSE](LICENSE). It does not impose any
additional licensing restriction.

The data and map assets bundled in this repository come from the sources below. All of
them permit free use and redistribution; the project deliberately avoids datasets
carrying a non-commercial clause.

Historical sources (public domain)
  *Records of the Three Kingdoms* (三国志), compiled by Chen Shou (陈寿), with
    commentary by Pei Songzhi (裴松之)
  *Book of the Later Han* (后汉书), compiled by Fan Ye (范晔), with commentary by Li
    Xian (李贤); the thirty treatise chapters are Sima Biao's (司马彪) *Continued Book
    of Han* (续汉书) (commandery establishment follows the *Treatise on Commanderies
    and Kingdoms* (郡国志))
  *Zizhi Tongjian* (资治通鉴), *Chronicles of Huayang* (华阳国志), *Commentary on the
    Water Classic* (水经注), *Book of Jin* (晋书), *Book of Han* (汉书)

Terrain and waterways (public domain / open data)
  AWS Terrain Tiles (terrarium) — a compilation of SRTM / ASTER GDEM / GMTED2010 /
    ETOPO1, AWS Open Data, no usage restrictions
  Natural Earth — coastline ne_10m_coastline, rivers
    ne_10m_rivers_lake_centerlines
  Natural Earth II — vegetation base colour

Commandery seat and stronghold coordinates
  Derived by reverse lookup from modern place names, each annotated with a certainty
  and a source citation; freely usable.

**Deliberately not used**
  CHGIS / Fudan TGAZ are CC BY-NC (non-commercial use only). This project used them
  during the research phase only, to cross-check coordinates; **no data from them is
  bundled in**. The David Rumsey historical map collection is likewise CC BY-NC-SA and
  was used only as a research reference. If you redistribute this repository, neither
  of them constitutes a restriction.
