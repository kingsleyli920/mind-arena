# Per-Faction AI and Long-Run Strategy Acceptance · 2026-09-28

## Product direction and the scope of this round

The goal is a Three Kingdoms strategy game: a player or an AI controls a faction and makes decisions about territory, armies, supply, officers, domestic administration and diplomacy. Model evals exist to check game quality and decision quality.

This round filled in per-faction controllers, pure spectator mode, cross-season memory across saves, and call limits; it did not adjust any combat numbers, and nothing was deployed.

Entry points: `app/play/MatchSetup.tsx`, `app/play/SessionPanel.tsx`, `lib/game/session.ts`. The UI and the headless long runs share `advanceSeason`.

## Zero-cost long runs

Run `npm run playtest`. The runtime data is `public/game/runtime-194.json`; 64 seasons per run, every faction using the engine's existing bot profiles. The simulation client is forbidden to call models, and the model budget is zero.

192 seasons in total, zero model decisions; all three runs ended at the season cap, with no unification winner.

- Marches, sieges, city captures, prisoners, supply cut-offs and faction annihilations all occurred; the strategic state kept changing.
- The first run converged from a many-sided melee to five powers; the other two still had a dozen-odd factions late on.
- The three runs formed alliances 428, 639 and 648 times and broke them 373, 555 and 566 times. Whether diplomacy this frequent produces meaningful strategic commitments needs a separate analysis of the rules and of bot behaviour.
- This data shows the engine can run a long game. It does not prove long-term balance, how fun the game is, or the strategic ability of models. The next step is to examine the late-game expansion bottleneck, alliance durations and the unification condition, and only then decide whether to adjust numbers.

## Cost and save-resume boundaries

- The model-decision budget is persisted before any request goes out; when it is not enough to cover every active model seat for the season, no request is sent for the entire season.
- A single decision makes at most two upstream requests, and the browser-side output cap is whichever of 4000/8000/16000 tokens was selected, reasoning included; the auto-learned budget cannot exceed it.
- The budget is not a dollar limit, nor an account-wide limit. Timed-out or failed requests may still be billed; costs the provider did not report back must not be treated as zero.
- A normal save/resume preserves the controllers, the player's pending orders, the model strategy plans, the previous round's dropped orders, the log, the budget and the observed faction; auto-spectate comes back paused.
- Refreshing while a model is still responding returns you to the last resolved position and keeps the reserved budget; that season's unsubmitted response may be lost. There is no promise that a mid-flight disconnect can reattach to the same model request.
- The save version goes up to 7; older versions are refused on restore, per the existing policy.

## A small real-model check

Driven for real through local `/play` → `/api/openrouter/chat` → OpenRouter. Pure spectator mode: Liu Biao controlled by `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, Liu Zhang by `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash`, every other faction a bot. Low reasoning tier, 8000 output tokens, at most 2 requests per decision, a budget of 4 for this run.

| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | Liu Biao / GLM Flash | 9 / 0 | 1 | 70.8 s | 0.000850 |
| 1 | Liu Zhang / DeepSeek Flash | 12 / 2 | 2 | 152.1 s | 0.001421 |
| 2 | Liu Biao / GLM Flash | 13 / 0 | 1 | 12.0 s | 0.002805 |

4 decisions and 5 upstream requests in total, reported cost **$0.007242**. Costs not reported during retries are not in that total and must not be treated as zero; the provider's bill is the authoritative amount.

Refreshed and resumed after season 1, confirming that the model assignments, strategy plans, log, observed faction and the 2/4 budget all came back, and in a paused state. Season 2 used auto-spectate; after resolution it reached 4/4 and stopped automatically, with both the advance and auto-spectate buttons disabled. No extra budget was added, and there was no all-model long run.

The two seats had different strategy plans and both carried their original direction into season 2: Liu Biao planned to take Jiaozhou in hand, Liu Zhang planned to take Yizhou commandery (益州郡) and restore southern supply. They do have observable cross-season plans, but two seasons is not enough to judge strategic ability, and the text of a plan is not the same as the plan succeeding. After checking season 1, a log-cursor bug in the old UI was fixed, and a regression test confirmed that later model observations include the previous season's battle report.
